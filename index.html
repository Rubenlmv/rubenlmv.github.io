Ruben Mamani‑Velasco

Noncommutative Algebra · Representation Theory

PhD candidate in pure mathematics at the University of Toledo. My research lies in noncommutative algebra and representation theory, specifically studying generalized Weyl algebras over rings with nilpotent elements. I investigate how classical theorems in category 𝒪 and Hochschild cohomology adapt when nilpotent elements are present.
